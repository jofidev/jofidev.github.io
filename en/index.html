Introduction

Language: Español | English

PhD in Computer Science, lecturer, and researcher with experience in artificial intelligence, educational chatbots, software engineering, and technologies applied to education.

His work connects computer science research with teaching practice. He currently carries out teaching and research activities at the National Autonomous University of Mexico and the Metropolitan Autonomous University, Iztapalapa Campus.
